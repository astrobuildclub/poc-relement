### 2. Intro / Mission

**Title**  
Renewable aromatics. Ready for today’s industry.

**Copy**  
Relement develops specialty biobased aromatic chemicals that replace fossil-based aromatics without compromising performance.

Our products deliver gloss, durability, stability and scratch resistance — while significantly improving sustainability.  
A missing element for products that are hard to recycle.

All elements matter.

### 3. Value proposition

**Performance**  
Better end-product performance compared to fossil aromatics.

**Sustainability**  
Non-food, biobased feedstocks with lower environmental impact.

**Scalability**  
Designed for industrial production and real-world applications.

### 4. Product highlight — bio MPA

**Title**  
bio MPA — our first commercial product

**Copy**  
bio MPA (3-methylphthalic anhydride) is a unique biobased aromatic with an optimally positioned methyl group — only accessible from non-edible biomass.

Successfully piloted and now entering industrial production.

**Key facts**
- Application: coatings, resins, materials  
- Status: industrial production contracted  
- Availability: samples & ton-scale volumes  

**CTA**  
Request a bio MPA sample
